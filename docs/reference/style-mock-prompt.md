Project Crookedile — a Filipino political-satire persuasion-battler where every opponent is a
potential convert, not just a target. You stand at a podium, back to camera, working a live
crowd: a single row of opponents behind a raised panel, each tracked by their own signed
Aggression meter — swing it positive and they turn hostile, attacking your shared Audience
Approval bar; swing it negative and they turn receptive and shield you. But never convert the
whole room: an all-receptive echo chamber halves your gains and bleeds your lead each turn —
you always want one villain left standing, and provoking one on purpose pays you a bonus card
draw. The Audience Approval bar is the only health in the game — no enemy HP. It is guarded by
two directional shields: your golden Support absorbs their attacks, their crimson Denial blocks
your gains. Reach 100 before it hits 0, or survive to the final turn holding a majority.

Each turn: 3 Energy and a hand of rhetoric cards in painterly frames color-coded by function —
green persuasion (Pray, Sermon), red aggression (Rebuke, Call Out Sin), status-stackers that
brand an opponent with Guilt, Shame, or Doubt (Guilt Trip, Name and Shame, Sow Doubt): stack any
three on one enemy and they crack — converting into a Fanatic for one turn, pumping the Approval
bar, then burning out Jaded and harder to convert again. Opponents telegraph revealed intents on
panels beneath their portraits — condemn the crowd against you, shield the meter, rally their
neighbors' aggression, absolve allies of your statuses — and a receptive ally pushed hostile
betrays you as a Turncoat, hitting harder because they knew your strategy.

Art direction: dark painterly realism, digital-oil, committee-hearing framing, one warm key
light on the speaker, crowd in near-black vignette. Charcoal and dark-walnut UI panels with thin
muted-gold chrome; parchment-scrap tutorial notes with handwriting and a crocodile doodle; toxic
green for approval, ember red for hostility, gold-amber for support. Filipino political flavor:
campaign-poster sheen, barong-clad opportunists, a megachurch preacher, a dynasty scion, a
celebrity in designer sunglasses.

## v6 — hand-drawn satire (CURRENT DIRECTION, 2026-11-07)

Project Crookedile — a Filipino political-satire persuasion-battler. You stand at a podium, back to camera, working a
committee hearing: a row of opponents behind a raised wooden panel, a packed public gallery behind them.

Hand-drawn 2D: confident thick ink outlines with varied weight, characters drawn like an animated cartoon
with strong expressive acting and clear silhouettes, set against softly painted atmospheric backgrounds with
visible brushwork. Rounded, appealing anthropomorphic animals with a sly satirical edge: likeable on the
surface, shady underneath. Warm lighting, rich but slightly muted colour: mustard, teal, brick red, cream,
olive. Filipino political satire.

UI: clean and slightly illustrated, one bold condensed sans-serif.

NOT photoreal, NOT 3D, NOT anime, NOT flat vector, NOT grimdark.
