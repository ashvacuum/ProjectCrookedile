# Crookedile — BGM prompts

*Odd Taxi cool as the shared DNA: deadpan, jazzy, political, wry. Two moods — the map wanders, combat struts.*

---

## 1. Meta map / overworld — laid-back nu-jazz

*The wandering theme. Chill, unbothered, a serious undercurrent. You nod to it between fights.*

> Laid-back nu-jazz lo-fi hip-hop, deadpan and cool, in the spirit of the Odd Taxi soundtrack.
> Mid-tempo head-nod groove around 88 BPM. Walking upright bass, brushed lo-fi drums with a dusty
> rimshot, warm Rhodes electric piano, a muted deadpan trumpet trading with a smoky tenor sax,
> soft vibraphone. Filipino flavor woven in lightly: a shimmering kulintang gong melody and a
> nylon-string guitar figure (a sly nod to kundiman/harana), never on-the-nose. Minor-key,
> smoke-filled mood — playful up top, quietly tense underneath. Sparse, loopable, no vocals.

---

## 2. Combat / battle — driving swagger (the focus)

*Reference: the swaggering strut of "Greenback Boogie" (Suits) + the propulsive modern cool of Dispatch. Same jazz-noir world as the map, but now you're the crocodile working the room with confidence and the clock is running. Whimsy from the strut and attitude; seriousness from the driving tension. Cocky, not frantic.*

> Driving, swaggering blues-funk with a modern hip-hop backbone — confident power-player strut in
> the vein of "Greenback Boogie" (Suits theme) crossed with the propulsive cool of the Dispatch
> soundtrack, still carrying Odd Taxi jazz-noir DNA. ~112 BPM, a strutting pocket groove. Dirty
> overdriven electric guitar riff, thick funky electric bass, punchy live drums with attitude,
> stabbing brass section (trumpet + sax hits), a growling organ, tambourine/handclaps for swagger.
> Filipino flavor as rhythmic punctuation: kulintang and brass stabs trading a syncopated Manila-
> sound hook. Minor-key, cocky and sly with a live tension underneath — the room is turning and
> you know it. Loopable, no vocals. Struts, doesn't panic.

**Composer notes:** ~108–116 BPM, minor/Dorian. Keep the map theme's instruments *present* (Rhodes,
kulintang, sax) so map→battle feels like the same world leaning in, not a genre change — combat
just adds guitar, live drums, and brass drive. Silence still allowed; swagger needs space to strut.
